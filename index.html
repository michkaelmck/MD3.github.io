    ABOUT ME

    Michkael Jude McKenzie is a Political Science major, Caribbean Studies major at Howard University from Hartford, Connecticut. Michkael is of Jamaican and Dominican Ancestry; his maternal family belongs to the Land of Wood and Water while his paternal family comes from The Nature Isle of the Caribbean. Michkael enjoys the art of storytelling whether this is through his works in the arts through trumpet/piano, dance, singing, or even writing/reciting poetry. Michkael is aiming for a PHD in sociology, seeking to answer the question of "Why Black Boys Are Not Reading More?" Through telling the story of Jamaica through its folklore and history. Michkael previously served as President of the Hartford Youth Advisory Board(the Mayor’s personal youth council), where he successfully championed youth rights and organized scholarship events to encourage students toward higher education. His commitment to social justice was further solidified by his participation in the ACLU Summer 2024 Program. Beyond his scholarly and civic commitments, Michkael is a passionate creative. He is an award-winning poet/essayist(Hartford Creative Contest), a co-author of the Thoughtful Citizen Handbook, and a dedicated music enthusiast who plays the trumpet and piano, with a special love for Jazz. His love for storytelling is fueled by fantasy literature, with Tomi Adeyemi's Children of Blood and Bone as a favorite, and his top movie choice is "They Cloned Tyrone." Michkael will be a champion of social advoacy and global change; he will change the world.

    PROJECT I

    This project utilizes Python and pandas to systematically clean and enhance a dataset of 50,000 mobile phone reviews, beginning with the standardization of customer names, brands, and languages while removing duplicates. It enforces data integrity by validating logical consistency, such as cross-referencing exchange rates with local prices and ensuring review dates are chronologically valid. Beyond basic cleaning, the workflow includes significant feature engineering, such as converting verified purchase status into binary data and categorizing review lengths for easier sorting. Finally, the project prepares the data for downstream analysis by deriving new metrics, including aggregated average ratings and numeric sentiment scores based on textual feedback.
